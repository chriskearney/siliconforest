## What a person would notice first (FXQA first pass 22:38Z–00:36Z; installed build above)

1. Click the ≡ menu button and the whole Firefox window disappears, every time (FX-022, S1, 1 click on a fresh window); the
   app keeps running with nothing on screen, and a playing video keeps SOUNDING with no window (reproduced 00:31Z). It also
   happened by itself while typing a URL and on a freshly started instance. This is the owner's no-window + sound report.
2. Sometimes Firefox never opens a window at launch (FX-020, S1, 2 of ~22): the GPU device open hangs; the same hang froze
   the whole desktop at 23:20Z (devfs DRM slots used up). It came back at 00:33Z, 57 min after the reboot.
3. Pictures go stale: after scrolling, old images stay over the tab strip/URL bar or page controls (FX-011, 3/8 sites),
   Wikipedia's banner half-painted with garbage stripes (FX-012), a corner-drag resize leaves the left 1024 px blank and
   does not resize (FX-033), a URL launch shows an empty "New Tab" picture for seconds (FX-036). All share the window's
   left 1024-px column.
4. Video plays without drops but reaches the screen at 25 updates/s for 60 and 30 fps clips (FX-035); interacting with the
   page drops frames and stalls up to 280 ms.
5. nytimes.com navigation has wrong letters (FX-010; TERM8 fix in test). Emoji are black-and-white, flags are letters
   (FX-024); Thai is boxes (FX-025).
6. Maximize slides the window under the left dock strip, hiding Back and the "File" menu (FX-034).
7. An idle CNN tab keeps ~0.9 core busy (FX-029); a static page idles at 6.6 % (FX-031); tab switches ~120 ms (FX-030).
8. Every launch leaves a crash report (FX-003); Print/Save Page As greyed (FX-026); file panel in the screen corner
   ignores Esc (FX-027); the dock cannot bring a windowless Firefox back — "Show" restarts it with the default profile
   (FX-032).
9. Works well: crisp text on CNN (owner's original no-text bug fixed), BBC, Guardian, Wikipedia + maths, GitHub, Reddit,
   YouTube, Amazon, Google search, Google sign-in; select popup, context menu, URL suggestions, copy/paste, menu strip,
   Command-Q sheet, full screen, minimise, new/private window, settings, closing the window while YouTube plays (sound
   stops within 0.3 s), quitting through the app menu.
10. Not yet covered (paused 00:36Z for the GPU device hang): x.com, SO, HN, apple, Google Fonts/Maps/Docs, figma, WebGL,
   CSS animation, emoji page, MDN form, ja/ar Wikipedia, long-page scroll on CNN, 30-min soak, YouTube 1080p60 in-page
   stats, minimise→restore, re-verification of fixes.
